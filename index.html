Privacy Policy

Transparency on how Hybra Void handles, processes, and safeguards technical identifiers across Discord servers.

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • GDPR Aligned
01

Commitment to Privacy

Your privacy is foundational to the Hybra Void architecture. This Privacy Policy outlines what minimal data is collected when you interact with Hybra Void on Discord, how it is processed in real time, how it is secured, and your rights regarding data deletion.

02

Information We Collect

Hybra Void follows a strict data-minimization standard, storing only technical identifiers required for core bot functionality:

  • Discord Identifiers: User IDs, Guild (Server) IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs.
  • Server Configurations: Designated log channels, FireProof shield states, AI moderation toggles, and TempVoice hub setups.
  • Activity & Progression: Warning counts, XP levels, virtual currency balances (Hybra Credits), daily cooldown timestamps, and invite statistics.

We never collect passwords, emails, IP addresses, payment info, or private DMs outside of active support tickets.

03

Message Content & AI Processing

To operate automated profanity filtering and interactive AI commands (/ask, /roast, and bot mentions), message text is securely transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to our AI inference provider (Groq API). Message content is evaluated ephemerally in real time and is never permanently stored or logged in our databases. Flagged violations may be forwarded solely to your server's private administrator log channel.

04

Data Storage & Security Safeguards

Persistent configuration and progression data are stored on isolated server environments with strict administrative access controls. We employ technical safeguards against unauthorized access or alteration and never sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

05

Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Data is retained only while Hybra Void remains active in a server or as needed to maintain your profile. You retain the full right to:

  • Request an export or summary of technical records linked to your Discord User ID.
  • Request permanent erasure of your stored warnings, XP, credits, or server configurations.

To submit a data request, open a ticket in our official HYBRA'S RISE Discord Server; requests are fulfilled within 72 hours.

06

Children's Privacy

In compliance with Discord's Terms of Service, Hybra Void is not directed toward individuals under the minimum digital consent age in their jurisdiction (typically 13 years old). We do not knowingly collect data from underage users.

07

Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy as new modules and commands are introduced. All updates will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
